Catalyzing Transformative STEM Learning

At the CATALYST Lab, we advance innovative and inclusive STEM education through interdisciplinary research on teaching, learning, and student development across educational settings. Our work explores how evidence-based pedagogy, experiential learning, computational simulations, AI, and emerging technologies can deepen conceptual understanding, strengthen STEM identity and motivation, and expand meaningful opportunities for students to engage and succeed in STEM.

Through collaborative research, student mentorship, and cross-disciplinary partnerships, the CATALYST Lab seeks to catalyze meaningful and lasting transformation in STEM education.

Research Pillars

Educational Tech

Simulations & Digital Learning

Investigating how dynamic visual tools, interactive simulations, and modeling software enhance conceptual understanding and engagement in STEM disciplines.

Pedagogy & Access

Inclusive Teaching Methods

Developing evidence-based instructional frameworks that foster collaborative learning, inquiry-based problem solving, and equitable STEM participation.

Teacher Preparation

Educator Growth & Development

Equipping pre-service and in-service educators with modern pedagogical tools, technology integration frameworks, and active learning strategies.
