Empowering Youth Through Active STEM Learning

The CATALYST Lab at California State University, Los Angeles serves as a research hub dedicated to driving STEM education transformation. We examine how innovative teaching strategies, educational technologies, and collaborative learning environments broaden STEM access and improve learning outcomes for youth and pre-service educators.

Our Core Mission

By bringing hands-on experimentation, interactive simulations, and evidence-based pedagogy into K–12 and postsecondary learning spaces, our goal is to foster deep conceptual understanding and inspire the next generation of STEM innovators.

Research Pillars

Educational Tech

Simulations & Digital Learning

Investigating how dynamic visual tools, interactive simulations, and modeling software enhance conceptual understanding and engagement in STEM disciplines.

Pedagogy & Access

Inclusive Teaching Methods

Developing evidence-based instructional frameworks that foster collaborative learning, inquiry-based problem solving, and equitable STEM participation.

Teacher Preparation

Educator Growth & Development

Equipping pre-service and in-service educators with modern pedagogical tools, technology integration frameworks, and active learning strategies.
